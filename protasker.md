# ProTasker App Summary + Claude Prompt

## What ProTasker Actually Is

ProTasker is **not** a generic task app. It is a **two-sided service marketplace** built in Flutter that connects:

- **Clients** who need help with home or professional services
- **Service Providers** who offer those services and manage bookings

### Core things the app does

- Lets clients browse services and providers by category
- Lets clients post a problem/job with title, description, photos, and location
- Lets clients request or book a provider
- Lets both sides chat in real time inside the app
- Shows booking progress and status updates like pending, accepted, on-the-way, in-progress, completed, and rejected
- Lets clients leave reviews and ratings after completion
- Gives providers a dashboard for requests, active jobs, upcoming jobs, and earnings
- Supports provider profiles, service areas, and job feed discovery
- Uses location-aware matching and nearby provider discovery
- Includes notifications, authentication, onboarding, and role-based app flows

### Good landing-page angles

- Trustworthy local service marketplace
- Fast way to find help nearby
- Easy way for providers to earn
- Clean, modern, mobile-first experience
- Real-time chat and booking tracking
